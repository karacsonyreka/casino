CASSINO

the Hungarian two-player Kaszinó — you against the computer

How to play

Goal

Capture as many valuable cards as you can from the table. Points are tallied once the whole deck has been played.

Card values

Number cards (2–10) are worth their face value. The four figure cards don't show a number on the card itself, but each still has a fixed value for capturing:

AAce (Ász)= 1
JJack (Alsó / Bubi)= 11
QQueen (Felső / Dáma)= 12
KKing (Király)= 13
Example: a King is worth 13, so it captures any table cards that add up to 13 — a single King, or e.g. a 4 + 9, or a Jack + 2 (11 + 2).

Your turn

Play exactly one card from your hand. There are two things you can do with it:

  • Place it — put it face up on the table, adding to the cards already there.
  • Capture — take one or more table cards whose values add up to the value of that single card.
Example: an 8 captures a single 8 on the table, or a 5 + 3 on the table (they add up to 8).

In this table, click a card from your hand to select it (clicking another hand card swaps the selection — only one card is played per turn). If you also want to capture, click the matching table card(s) too — the status line tells you once the selection adds up to a legal move. Then press Play.

Sweeps

If your capture takes every card currently on the table, that's a sweep — worth an extra point at the end of the deal.

New rounds

Each player starts with 3 cards. Once both hands are empty, 3 fresh cards are dealt to each player from the stock (if any remain), and whoever captured most recently leads the new round.

End of the deal

When the stock runs out and both hands are finally empty, any cards still left on the table go to whoever captured last.

Scoring

  • 3 points for capturing the majority of the 52 cards (27+)
  • 2 points for capturing the majority of spades (7+)
  • 1 point for each ace you capture
  • 2 points for capturing the 10 of diamonds ("Big Cassino")
  • 1 point for capturing the 2 of spades ("Little Cassino")
  • 1 point per sweep

Highest total wins the deal.
